# Research roadmap

## Phase 0 — evidence-controlled baseline (complete)

Establish the question, claim boundary, source register, incident chronology and fail-closed gates. Completion does not imply that event extent is known.

## Phase 1 — terrain–rainfall–observation feasibility (HOLD)

Deliver reproducible terrain susceptibility and candidate catchments; audit settlement uncertainty, precipitation availability and post-event satellite observations; record every blocker. Phase 1 remains **HOLD** until a validated regional precipitation asset is acquired or a verified station record is obtained. The terrain component is usable only as modeled susceptibility.

Opening criteria for a Phase 1 GO:

- authenticated, checksummed IMERG Late (then Final when available) or verified station observations;
- explicit accumulation windows tied to the provisional event-time envelope;
- independent validation of at least one pour point or impact crossing;
- continued fail-closed satellite decision.

## Phase 2 — event-evidence envelope (not authorized)

Only start if a compatible post-event observation and pre-event baseline pass every condition in `docs/SENSOR_GATE.md`. Outputs must retain observed/probable/possible/insufficient classes. A binary flood polygon is not the target.

## Phase 3 — partial exposure screening (not authorized)

Only start after an event-evidence envelope passes validation and receptor completeness is characterized. Report potential exposure, never allocate aggregate dwelling reports to footprints, and withhold totals when completeness is unknown.

## Permanently gated without new inputs

Hydraulic depth, velocity, discharge, hydrographs, return periods, economic loss and climate attribution remain unavailable or out of scope. Machine learning has no role without relevant labels and independent validation.
