# Limitations and prohibited interpretations

## Live incident reporting

Operational casualty, injury, missing-person and dwelling figures changed during the response. The repository preserves report versions; it does not certify a final humanitarian census.

## Spatial support

Settlement coordinates are approximate centroids. They are not damage points. Historical 2016 population figures are context only and must not be presented as 2026 population.

## Satellite timing

The event was a flash flood. Water may have receded before a post-event acquisition. A non-detection would not prove that flooding did not occur.

## Terrain resolution

Thirty-metre elevation data cannot resolve individual streets, walls, drainage structures or water depth around buildings. Hydrological conditioning changes modeled pathways and must be reported.

## Precipitation resolution

Satellite precipitation estimates provide regional context. They cannot establish the peak intensity at a named village without local validation.

## Exposure data

Open building and road datasets may be incomplete or outdated. Intersections represent potential exposure, not observed damage.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not infer accuracy from pixel size.
- Do not label all low radar backscatter as floodwater.
- Do not compare incompatible radar geometries without a justified harmonization method.
- Do not allocate aggregate reported damage to mapped structures.
- Do not use machine learning without relevant labels and independent validation.
- Do not infer climate attribution from this event reconstruction.
- Do not publish sensitive household or victim information.
